Flood safety — before, during, after

The short version that saves lives. Emergency: 112

Before

  • Know your nearest higher ground and shelter
  • Kit: torch, radio, power bank, 3 days water, dry food, medicines, documents in waterproof bag
  • Charge phones when heavy rain is forecast

During

  • Never walk or drive through floodwater — 6 inches knocks you down, 2 feet floats a car
  • Stay away from rivers, drains, culverts, power lines
  • Water entering home: switch off mains, move up, call 112

After

  • Return only when authorities say safe
  • Boil drinking water — disease spikes after floods
  • Electrician checks anything submerged before use
