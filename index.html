Nepal floods — downstream impact in UP & Bihar

The 26 August 2026 flash floods in Nepal (Bhotekoshi/Trishuli rivers) were among the deadliest in the country's history. The Gandak river carried debris and remains downstream into Kushinagar and Maharajganj districts of Uttar Pradesh, and authorities in eastern UP and north Bihar remain on vigil for river levels during the ongoing monsoon withdrawal phase.

Flood safety

Do not walk or drive through waterlogged roads — six inches of moving water can knock you down, two feet can float a car. Stay clear of swollen rivers, drains and culverts. See the complete flood safety guide.
