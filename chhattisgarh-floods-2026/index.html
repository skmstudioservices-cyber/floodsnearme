Chhattisgarh Floods 2026 — Live Updates & Affected Areas

Heavy rain & flood-like situation · 25–28 September 2026
✅ Last updated: 26 September 2026 — situation developing. IMD forecast: light-to-moderate rain across most of the state with very heavy falls possible at isolated places in 13 districts through Sunday. Check official advisories before travel.

⚠️ Current situation (as reported, 26 September)

  • 2 rain-related deaths — a 40-year-old farmer drowned in a flooded drain in Kondagaon (25 Sep); a woman died when a mud wall collapsed in Bastar district's Kurandi area
  • Statewide school holiday declared for Saturday 26 September — all government, private and aided schools
  • Sabari river above danger mark at Konta (Sukma): 43.02 m vs danger level 42 m (warning level 40.50 m) — high alert issued
  • NH-30 submerged near Dimrapal (Jagdalpur) — traffic between Raipur and Jagdalpur disrupted
  • Villages cut off in Sukma and Bijapur; Sukma–Malkangiri road blocked at Jhapra bridge
  • Indravati river alert for low-lying and riverside areas of Jagdalpur
  • 48 passengers rescued from a bus trapped in floodwater near Sarai Bhadar, Gariaband — all safe
  • Girl students evacuated from Kanya Bhejji Ashram (Sukma) as the Sabari rose

Holiday declared in 8 districts (government and private schools): Dantewada, Bijapur, Kondagaon, Kanker, Bastar, Gariaband, Dhamtari and Durg. Quarterly exams in government schools postponed in affected areas. On 26 September, the Chief Minister announced a statewide holiday for all schools. Follow your district administration's announcements for Monday.

IMD alert — 13 districts

The Meteorological Centre has warned of light-to-moderate rain across most of the state with very heavy rainfall at isolated places over the next three days, with a deep depression expected to bring showers to all districts through Sunday. Districts under the heavy-rain alert: Balod, Baloda Bazar, Bemetara, Dhamtari, Durg, Gariaband, Kabirdham, Kanker, Khairagarh-Chhuikhadan-Gandai, Kondagaon, Mahasamund, Raipur and Rajnandgaon.

Safety advice

  • Do not venture near swollen rivers, streams, culverts or inundated areas
  • Do not attempt to cross flooded bridges or roads — turn around, use official diversions
  • Avoid unnecessary travel in the 13 alert districts until Sunday
  • Keep phones charged, documents and medicines dry and ready
  • If water enters your home, move to higher ground and call 112
